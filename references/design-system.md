# Visual system distilled from the final BP

`assets/reference.pdf` is the sole visual truth. It is a 23-page A4 portrait editorial BP. `assets/reference-contact.png` must be the freshly rendered contact sheet from that file, never a stale earlier draft.

## 1. Character

This is `quiet field archive × contemporary editorial report`: warm paper, light Song-style display type, fine black rules, mono index furniture, disciplined tables, sparse red/brass accents, and generous pauses. It should feel closer to an independent cultural journal or exhibition catalogue than a competition template.

The final is **not** white-background corporate reporting, colored-table business consulting, a SaaS card grid, or an infographic booklet.

Avoid: bold sans-serif page titles, white canvas, boxed callouts, colored table headers, zebra fills, rounded cards, shadows, gradients, icon sets, heavy chart chrome, tourism-brochure imagery, red-gold heritage styling, and decorative dashboards.

## 2. Measured constants

- Format: A4 portrait, `210 × 297 mm`.
- Full-page background: bone `#F3F0E8`.
- Main content: from `18 mm` left/right; about `25–27 mm` below the top edge.
- Running header: top about `14 mm`; thin rule spans the full content width.
- Content width: about `174 mm`.
- Grid: 12 columns; ordinary layouts resolve to full width, 2/10, 3/9, 4/8, or 3 equal columns.
- Spacing rhythm: `2 / 4 / 8 / 12 mm`.
- Rules: hairline `0.35–0.55 pt`; key top/bottom table rules `0.8–1 pt`.
- Corners: square. Ordinary content has no boxes or radius.

## 3. Exact palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#171717` | titles, body, rules, primary bands |
| Bone | `#F3F0E8` | every page background and reversed text on dark bands |
| Oxide red | `#8E382D` | index numbers, key judgments, short vertical rules |
| Archive gray | `#8A8578` | English index, secondary labels, captions, sources |
| Aged brass | `#B18B4F` | metric underline, process nodes, rare mono labels |
| Oxidized teal | `#3E6863` | one secondary structural layer or emphasized band |
| Wood brown | `#765A45` | rare photo-derived/cultural accent |

Most pages use only bone, ink, gray, and one tiny accent. Red/brass/teal are information roles, not a palette to spread evenly.

## 4. Typography measured from the PDF

The final embeds Noto Serif SC ExtraLight, Noto Sans SC Thin, IBM Plex Mono Regular, and IBM Plex Mono SemiBold.

- Display Chinese: `Noto Serif SC`, `Source Han Serif SC`, `SimSun`, serif. Use a light weight (`200–300`), never bold.
- Body Chinese: `Noto Sans SC`, `Source Han Sans SC`, `Microsoft YaHei`, sans-serif. Use `300–400`.
- English, index, numbers, dates: `IBM Plex Mono`, `Source Code Pro`, Consolas, monospace.

| Role | Final measurement | Treatment |
|---|---:|---|
| Cover project name | `64 pt` | light serif, tight leading |
| Interior conclusion title | `20 pt` | light serif, 1.5 line height when two lines |
| Closing project name | `40 pt` | light serif |
| Red closing statement | `13.5 pt` | light serif |
| Key judgment | `14 pt` | light serif, oxide red, left rule |
| Section/table title | `12 pt` | sans, normal/medium |
| Body | `10.6–10.8 pt` | sans, line height about `1.7` |
| Table | `8.6–9.8 pt` | sans, generous row height |
| Main metric | `17 pt` | mono semibold |
| Running header index | `9 pt` | mono semibold, red |
| Running English/Chinese | `7.5 pt` | mono gray / sans ink |
| Source/footer | `7.5–8 pt` | gray |

Large light serif titles are the signature. If a new page title looks like a bold PowerPoint heading, it is wrong.

## 5. Running header

Every interior page begins with this exact visual grammar:

`02  SOLUTION  解决方案：一个 IP 中枢、两层叙事、三类产品                                                08`

- section number: red mono semibold;
- English section label: gray mono uppercase with tracking;
- Chinese running title: small sans in ink;
- page number: mono semibold at the far right;
- one thin ink rule directly below.

There is normally no conventional footer. Sources or notes sit just above a faint bottom rule when present.

## 6. Page archetypes in the final

### Editorial cover

Asymmetric 5/7 split. The left side carries a small archive index, a large 64 pt serif name, a small subtitle and a red manifesto. The right side is one tall, tightly cropped real photograph. A slim vertical mono archive label sits near the far left. Metadata anchors the lower left. Do not use a centered logo or abstract corporate panel.

### Executive summary

Two-line 20 pt serif thesis, one edited paragraph, four unboxed metrics spread horizontally, a ruled three-column progress table, then a red serif judgment with a short vertical rule. The lower page remains quiet.

### Editorial argument page

Two-line serif conclusion title, 1–2 paragraphs, then numbered statements or a short ruled structure. Section numbers may be brass/red mono. The layout relies on baseline rhythm and hairlines, not cards.

### Ruled table page

Tables resemble editorial ledgers: no outer box, no fill, no zebra striping. Use a heavier top and bottom rule, hairline header separator, generous row padding, and alignment by columns. The table title may include a gray inline note.

### Structural band page

For a genuinely important two-layer architecture only, use one ink band and one teal band with bone reversed text. Separate them with a very thin brass axis and five small brass nodes. This is an exception, not a default table style.

### Evidence or data page

Use one dominant image/chart, quiet labels, and editorial interpretation. Preserve warm paper around it. Real materials remain recognizable; do not turn them into thumbnail dashboards.

### Closing

Large empty bone field. A stack of nine short red rules at upper left; brass mono English label; 40 pt serif project name; thin red underline; 13.5 pt red mission sentence. Metadata sits near lower left; a small repeated rule motif and brass page number sit at lower right.

## 7. Metrics, tables, and charts

- Metrics are unboxed. Large mono number, small unit, gray label, then a 6–8 mm brass underline.
- Tables use rules only. Do not fill the header row.
- A conclusion or risk note uses a 0.5–0.75 mm red vertical rule and red serif lead-in, not a rectangle.
- Charts should look drawn into the paper: ink axes, gray guides, red focal series, teal comparison, brass forecast. No container, shadow, legend card, or colored plotting background.
- Use direct labels and a source line. Keep chart labels at 8.5 pt or larger.
- Dark/teal bands are reserved for a central architecture or a decisive contrast, not routine data.

## 8. Page rhythm

The book alternates among editorial thesis, ruled evidence, data/table, and rare structural-band pages. Large whitespace is normal. Dense pages remain calm because the type is light, the rules are thin, and no box competes with the title.

## 9. Fidelity test

Compare the new contact sheet with `assets/reference-contact.png` and selected pages of `assets/reference.pdf`:

- Is the entire canvas warm bone?
- Does every interior page begin with the index header and thin rule?
- Are page titles light serif at about 20 pt?
- Are most tables fill-free and rule-led?
- Are metrics unboxed?
- Is red sparse and structural?
- Does the document feel like a cultural editorial dossier rather than a business template?

Any `no` means the style is not yet faithful.
